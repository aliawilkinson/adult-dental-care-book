| Periodontal disease and tooth loss | NIDCR periodontal disease data/statistics (NHANES 2009-2014): https://www.nidcr.nih.gov/research/data-statistics/periodontal-disease ; CDC "About Periodontal (Gum) Disease": https://www.cdc.gov/oral-health/about/gum-periodontal-disease.html | US-specific surveillance data. | Interdental cleaning | Cochrane plus national guidelines | Distinguish floss, interdental brushes, and water flossers by evidence and use case. |
| Dental X-rays | American Dental Association / U.S. FDA, "Dental Radiographic Examinations: Recommendations for Patient Selection and Limiting Radiation Exposure," revised 2012: https://www.fda.gov/radiation-emitting-products/medical-x-ray-imaging/ada-fda-guide-patient-selection-dental-radiographs | US guideline: bitewings every 6-18 months for elevated caries risk, 24-36 months otherwise. Compare against non-US schedules before presenting as universal. |
| Dental emergencies | National emergency dental guidance | Needed for knocked-out tooth, abscess, swelling, pain, and escalation advice. |

## Nano-Hydroxyapatite and Remineralization

| Topic | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nHA caries prevention and remineralization | Pawinska M, Paszynska E, Amaechi BT, Meyer F, Enax J, Limeback H. "Clinical evidence of caries prevention by hydroxyapatite: An updated systematic review and meta-analysis." J Dent. 2024 Dec. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0300571224005992 | Reports expanding RCT/in situ evidence that hydroxyapatite reduces caries without fluoride. Co-author Enax is employed by Dr. Kurt Wolff GmbH & Co. KG, a hydroxyapatite toothpaste manufacturer. Disclose this affiliation wherever the review is cited. |
| nHA vs. fluoride | Systematic review and meta-analysis, "The role of hydroxyapatite-based, fluoride-free toothpastes on the prevention and remineralization of initial caries lesions." 2025. PMID 40107597. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0300571225001368 | Only 4 of 68 identified studies were eligible for inclusion. Found no significant difference vs. fluoride on lesion progression (DMFS/ICDAS), but did find differences in lesion size and fluorescence at 6 months. Do not invent superiority; represent as roughly comparable on some endpoints with a thin evidence base. |
| nHA mouthwash or tablets | Product-specific evidence if available | Do not assume toothpaste evidence transfers to all rinse/tablet products. Still no dedicated evidence located as of this pass; keep flagged in unresolved-evidence.md. |

## Source Intake Rules

- Add complete citations or links as research is performed.

- Record important limitations.
- Never use manufacturer marketing as sole support for clinical claims.
