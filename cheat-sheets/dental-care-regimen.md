# Dental Care Regimen

### Long-term enamel strength and remineralization

Pre-treatment foundation for veneers, whitening, sealants, and keeping your real teeth immaculate for as long as possible.

---

## Materials

- Copper tongue scraper
- Electric toothbrush + brush heads
- 10% nHA toothpaste
- nHA mouthwash tablets
- Remineralization drops and/or mouthwash
- Glide floss
- Water flosser
- Hydrogen peroxide, for tool cleaning only
- Mouth guard, if you grind

---

## The Golden Rule

**Never brush immediately after food, coffee, tea, soda, or lemon water.**

Acid softens enamel. Brushing right after scrubs the softened layer instead of protecting it. Wait, swish instead, brush later.

---

## Daily Routine

### Morning

1. Scrape tongue with copper scraper.
2. Drink water.
3. Brush with 10% nHA toothpaste.
4. Do not rinse, eat, or drink for 20-30 minutes.

That quiet window lets the nHA sit on your teeth and do its job.

### After Meals / Acidic Drinks

Coffee, tea, soda, lemon water, wine, sports drinks, and most snacks all count.

- Swish with remineralization mouthwash or drops first.
- If you do not have that, rinse well with plain water.
- Sugar-free gum is a good add-on because it gets saliva moving.
- Do not brush immediately, even after rinsing.

These tools shorten the recovery window. They do not erase it.

### Evening

Do this 30+ minutes after dinner.

1. Mouthwash.
2. Floss with Glide.
3. Brush with 10% nHA toothpaste.
4. Water flosser for the gumline and tooth curves.
5. Tongue scraper.
6. Leave it. No rinsing after.
7. Mouth guard, if you grind.

Night is the best remineralization window because the toothpaste can sit while you sleep.

---

## If You Must Brush Right After Coffee Or Food

Best case is waiting 30-60 minutes. If that is not happening because you are heading into a meeting, about to kiss someone, or simply need your mouth to feel normal:

1. Swish with remineralization mouthwash first, or plain water if that is what you have.
2. Wait at least 5-10 minutes.
3. Use nHA toothpaste, not whitening toothpaste, baking soda, charcoal, or anything gritty.
4. Use light pressure and a soft-bristle setting.
5. Keep it short: 60-90 seconds.

This is harm reduction, not a replacement for the Golden Rule.

## Maintenance Schedule

| Frequency | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | Soak tongue scraper, brush head, and water flosser tip in hydrogen peroxide for a few minutes. |
| Monthly | Replace toothbrush head. |
| Every 6 months | Dental cleaning and exam. |
| Yearly | Bitewing X-rays, unless your dentist recommends a different schedule. These catch decay between teeth and under existing fillings or crowns that a visual exam can miss. |
| Every 3-5 years or as needed | Full-mouth or panoramic X-rays for implants, wisdom teeth, orthodontics, or bigger-picture planning. |

---

## Whitening Protocol

Separate track. Different risk profile, different timeline.

- Carbamide peroxide 10% in a custom-fit tray.
- Use nightly for 7 days.
- Maintain at 1x/week.
- If sensitivity or gum irritation shows up, pause or drop to every other day before continuing.

Whitening is not part of the daily maintenance routine. It has its own start/stop logic.

## Cheat Sheet

### Morning

- Copper scraper
- Drink water
- Brush with nHA
- Wait 20-30 minutes before eating or drinking

### After Meals

- Rinse with remineralization mouthwash or water
- Chew sugar-free gum if you want
- Do not brush yet
- If you must brush: rinse, wait 5-10 minutes, nHA toothpaste, light pressure, short brush

### Evening

- Mouthwash
- Floss
- Brush with nHA
- Water flosser
- Copper scraper
- Leave it overnight
