# Source Index

Use this file to track sources that may support consequential claims in the manuscript. Prefer primary studies, systematic reviews, and official professional guidance.

## International Outcome and Burden Sources

| Topic | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Country oral-health profiles | World Health Organization oral-health country profiles | Starting point for country-level oral disease burden and system context. Verify data year and definitions. |
| Global disease burden | Global Burden of Disease oral-health research | Use for comparable modeled disease-burden estimates. Record modeled-data limitations. |
| Health-system comparison | OECD health and dental-care data where available | Useful for access, coverage, utilization, affordability, and system characteristics. |
| Evidence synthesis | Cochrane Oral Health | Prefer for intervention effectiveness where available. |

## National and Professional Guidance

| Topic | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home care basics | National guidance from multiple jurisdictions | Compare recommendations rather than treating any single country as universal. |
| Brushing timing after acidic foods/drinks | International guidance and primary evidence where available | Verify exact timing, evidence basis, and whether advice is consensus or extrapolated mechanism. |
| Interdental cleaning | Cochrane plus national guidelines | Distinguish floss, interdental brushes, and water flossers by evidence and use case. |
| Dental X-rays | National radiography guidelines | Compare risk-based schedules and avoid presenting one country's insurance habit as universal. |
| Dental emergencies | National emergency dental guidance | Needed for knocked-out tooth, abscess, swelling, pain, and escalation advice. |

## Nano-Hydroxyapatite and Remineralization

| Topic | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nHA caries prevention and remineralization | Systematic reviews and randomized trials | Track concentration, formulation, fluoride comparison, endpoints, and limitations. |
| nHA vs. fluoride | Comparative RCTs and reviews | Do not invent superiority. Represent equivalence, noninferiority, or uncertainty only when supported. |
| nHA mouthwash or tablets | Product-specific evidence if available | Do not assume toothpaste evidence transfers to all rinse/tablet products. |

## Cosmetic Dentistry

| Topic | Source | Notes |

| Veneers, crowns, bonding | Prosthodontic or restorative dentistry sources | Needed for longevity, indications, preparation, replacement timelines, and failure modes. |

## Source Intake Rules

- Add complete citations or links as research is performed.
- Note whether each source is human clinical evidence, laboratory evidence, guideline, review, or expert opinion.
- Record important limitations.
- Never use manufacturer marketing as sole support for clinical claims.
