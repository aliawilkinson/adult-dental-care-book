# International Outcomes Comparison

This file must be completed before drafting evidence-based recommendations.

## Purpose

Identify countries that perform strongly by specific oral-health metric. Do not declare one country as having "the best teeth."

## Metrics To Compare

- untreated caries in permanent teeth
- untreated caries in primary teeth
- DMFT or DMFS at comparable ages
- proportion of children who are caries-free
- severe periodontal disease
- complete tooth loss
- natural teeth retained in older adulthood
- oral-disease disability burden
- preventive dental-care access
- unmet need for dental treatment
- socioeconomic inequality in oral health
- affordability and public coverage
- frequency of invasive restorative intervention
- oral-cancer incidence and mortality where relevant

## Drafting Rule

Use this file to inform recommendations, but do not infer that a national practice caused a national outcome merely because they occur together.
