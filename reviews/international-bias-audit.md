# International Bias Audit

Use this review after research and drafting.

## Audit Questions

- Does the manuscript default unnecessarily to American guidance?
- Does it ignore strong non-American research?
- Does it treat American regulation as scientific truth?
- Does it romanticize international practices without evidence?
- Does it make invalid country comparisons?
- Does it confuse health-system policy with individual clinical evidence?
- Does it overlook disagreement between high-performing countries?
- Does it replace U.S. provincialism with a fantasy that Scandinavia, Japan, or Europe must be right about everything?
