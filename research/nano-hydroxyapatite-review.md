# Nano-Hydroxyapatite Review

Nano-hydroxyapatite must receive a serious international evidence review before the book makes strong claims about it.

## Required Coverage

- what fluoride is and how it works in toothpaste
- how fluoride and nHA mechanisms differ
- water fluoridation history, benefits, controversy, and risk claims
- whether concerns about fluoride plausibly map onto nHA, and where they do not
- Japanese research and usage history
- European research
- randomized clinical trials
- systematic reviews
- comparisons with fluoride toothpaste
- concentration differences
- formulation differences
- remineralization endpoints
- caries-prevention endpoints
- dentin-hypersensitivity endpoints
- white-spot lesion research
- in situ and laboratory findings
- safety and particle-characterization questions
- limitations of the evidence
- regulatory and professional-guideline differences

## Drafting Rule

Do not dismiss nHA because it is less established in American guidance. Do not overstate it because it is used internationally. Represent the evidence by outcome.
