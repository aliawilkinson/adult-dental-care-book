# Claim Map

This file should be expanded before and during drafting.

## Chapter Claim Tracking Template

For each chapter, track:

- consequential claim
- claim type: medical, scientific, behavioral, product, safety, regulatory, cost, cosmetic, or author preference
- current support
- source needed
- risk if wrong
- final manuscript handling

## Global High-Risk Claim Areas

1. nHA effectiveness, concentration, and comparison to fluoride.
2. Brushing timing after acidic exposure.
3. Gum disease symptoms and escalation guidance.
4. Cavity reversal vs. remineralization vs. irreversible decay.
5. Whitening safety and contraindications.
6. Veneer, crown, and bonding longevity and preparation claims.
7. Dental emergency instructions.
8. X-ray frequency and risk-based care.
9. Dental tourism guidance.

## International Requirements

For every consequential claim, ask:

- Is this advice shared internationally?
- Which countries recommend it?
- Which countries do something materially different?
- Are the differences based on evidence, regulation, economics, professional culture, or historical practice?
- Does the recommendation depend on local water fluoridation, diet, product concentration, insurance coverage, or access to professional care?
- Is this prevention, arrest, remineralization, symptom management, or restorative treatment?
- Are different countries using different definitions or diagnostic thresholds?

## Drafting Rule

Do not let a chapter proceed with unsupported consequential claims hidden in polished prose. Cite them or flag them.
