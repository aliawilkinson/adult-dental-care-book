# Quick Reference

Daily: scrape, hydrate, brush gently, rinse after meals instead of brushing immediately, clean between teeth, and leave nighttime toothpaste in place. Weekly: clean tools and inspect gums, tongue, and tooth edges. Monthly: replace worn brush heads and check your night guard. Yearly: review your risk level, dental visit schedule, X-ray rationale, cosmetic goals, and product setup.

Bleeding gums: improve plaque control and book an evaluation if bleeding persists, swelling appears, pockets are mentioned, or teeth feel mobile. Tooth pain: sensitivity that resolves quickly is different from lingering, spontaneous, or night-waking pain. Chipped tooth: smooth tiny chips can often wait briefly, but pain, looseness, exposed dentin, or a sharp fracture needs prompt care.

Product priority: start with a soft electric brush, credible toothpaste, floss or interdental brushes, and a water flosser if it helps you clean consistently. Add whitening, cosmetic tools, and luxury extras only after the foundation is stable.

## Goal-Specific Mini Checklists

### Maximum Enamel Reinforcement

Morning: scrape the tongue, drink water, brush gently with nHA toothpaste, spit, and do not rinse. Give the paste 20 to 30 quiet minutes if your morning allows it.

After meals or acidic drinks: rinse with water or a remineralizing rinse. Chew sugar-free gum if it helps saliva flow. Do not brush immediately.

Night: floss, brush, water floss if useful, scrape the tongue, and leave the toothpaste residue in place. If you grind, wear the guard.

### Cosmetic Brightening

Before whitening: confirm there is no untreated decay, major gum inflammation, cracked tooth pain, or restoration mismatch that will make results uneven.

During whitening: use the lowest effective protocol that produces the result you want. Back off for sensitivity or gum irritation.

After whitening: maintain with periodic touch-ups rather than living permanently in an active whitening cycle.

### Pre-Veneer Conditioning

Sixty days out: make the gumline healthy and boring.

Thirty days out: finish whitening natural teeth that need to match the planned work, if whitening is part of the plan.

## High-Control Shopping List

Start here if you want the full routine: electric toothbrush, replacement heads, floss that works for your contacts, water flosser, nHA toothpaste, portable rinse or drops for acidic meals, copper tongue scraper, and a tool-cleaning plan. Add a night guard only if you grind or your dentist recommends one. Add whitening only after the health foundation is stable.

Budget order: toothbrush and toothpaste first, interdental cleaning second, dental evaluation third, water flosser and convenience rinses after that. Expensive tools cannot compensate for never using the basic ones.
